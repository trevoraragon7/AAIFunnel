Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Aragon AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

This policy applies to this website and to information you provide when booking a call or contacting Aragon AI. Separate agreements may apply if you become a client and share business data for a project.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any message or scheduling details you provide.
  • Meeting information submitted through Calendly, such as your selected meeting time, invitee details, and answers to scheduling questions.
  • Project information you choose to share with us, such as workflow details, business needs, documents, screenshots, or examples related to a potential project.
  • Basic website and device information, such as IP address, browser type, referring page, pages visited, and timestamps, which may be collected by hosting, security, analytics, or scheduling tools.
  • Quiz answers shown on this website. The quiz is designed to run in your browser; we do not collect quiz answers unless you separately submit information through Calendly, email, a form, or another contact method.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Schedule and manage discovery calls, consultations, and follow-up conversations.
  • Respond to questions, requests, and potential project inquiries.
  • Understand your business needs and evaluate whether our services may be a good fit.
  • Provide, improve, secure, and troubleshoot this website and our services.
  • Send service-related communications or, if you have opted in or otherwise requested it, relevant business updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, users, clients, and services.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information or share it with data brokers. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, schedule meetings, communicate with you, or provide our services.

For scheduling, we use Calendly. When you book a meeting through Calendly, the information you provide is processed by Calendly according to Calendly's terms and privacy notice.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect legal rights, to prevent fraud or abuse, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website and third-party tools may use cookies, local storage, pixels, logs, or similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, measure performance, prevent abuse, or support scheduling. You can usually control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No website, email system, or online service can guarantee complete security, so please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for the project or request.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also have the right to opt out of certain uses of personal information, such as targeted advertising or sale, if those activities apply.

To make a privacy request, contact us at hello@aragonai.com. We may need to verify your request before responding.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or teens under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party websites or tools, including Calendly. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Please review their privacy notices before providing information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will apply going forward unless otherwise stated.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact Aragon AI at hello@aragonai.com.
