Marketing Agency

From Half a Day Per Proposal to 20 Minutes

The Problem

Luminary Creative was losing 3–4 hours per proposal. Scoping sessions would finish and a team member would spend the rest of the day writing a customized proposal from scratch. At 8–10 proposals a month, that's nearly a full work week lost.

What We Built

We built a proposal generator trained on their service catalog, past proposals, and pricing logic. After a scoping call, the team enters a few details and the system produces a complete, on-brand proposal — ready for review in under 5 minutes.

4 hrs → 20 min per proposal Proposal output up 3× Close rate improved
Real Estate

60% of Leads Were Never Getting a Response. Now Zero Go Unanswered.

The Problem

Summit Properties was generating 40–60 leads per month through their website. Nearly 60% never received a response — they came in after hours, on weekends, or got lost in a full inbox. Those were real people ready to buy, going to a competitor.

What We Built

We deployed an AI receptionist that responds to every inbound lead within 90 seconds — any time of day. It qualifies them with 3–4 questions, answers their initial questions, and books a showing or consultation directly into the agent's calendar.

0% leads left uncontacted 22% more qualified showings Avg. response time: 80 sec
